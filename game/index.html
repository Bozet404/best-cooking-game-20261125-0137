SIZZLE & SERVE

A tiny kitchen rush — take orders, chop, grill, plate, serve.

Orders land on the board up top. Run the kitchen: grab ingredients from the counters, chop lettuce and tomato at the board, grill patties on the stove, build each dish on the plating counter, and serve it at the window before the customer walks out. One day, three minutes, three hearts.

Move: WASD / arrows / click the floor  •  Use a station: E or click it  •  Hold E to chop & wash  •  P pause  •  M mute  •  R restart

An original game. No network, no accounts, no purchases — just a kitchen.

How to play

MoveWASD, arrow keys, or click the floor to walk there.
Take foodStand by a counter and press E (or click it). You carry up to 3 things.
ChopCarry raw lettuce or tomato to the Chopping Board and hold E.
GrillCarry a raw patty to the Stove, press E to put it on. Take it off when the bar turns green — leave it and it burns.
PlateAt the Plating Counter, press E to add the item on top of your stack. When the plate matches an order, press E again to pick the plate up.
ServeCarry the finished plate to the Serve Window and press E. Fast serves build a combo.
Fix mistakesTrash Bin throws things away. The Sink washes a burnt patty back to raw (hold E).

The menu

Classic BurgerBun + grilled patty + chopped lettuce — 18 coins
Cheesy MeltBun + grilled patty + cheese — 22 coins
Garden SaladChopped lettuce + chopped tomato — 12 coins
BruschettaBun + chopped tomato + cheese — 16 coins

Each order gives you 60 seconds. Let one expire and you lose a heart; lose all three hearts and the day ends early. The day lasts 3 minutes.

Tip: serve orders back-to-back to build a combo — up to double coins.
